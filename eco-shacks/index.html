01How do I become a pro member?

Join directly by subscribing: Become a pro member. Pro membership grants access to the R99k/month off-plan allocation, guest payment splitting, and exclusive reservations across The Hargrave bungalows.

02How does the R299k monthly payment structure work?

Purchasers contribute 99k each month off-plan over a 60-month (5-year) horizon with 0% interest. This provides an approachable, predictable entry into an ultra-prime oceanfront property starting from R29m without burdensome bank mortgage fees. Bungalows are allocated by preference on a first-come basis — priority goes to the first person to complete payment. After your initial booking payment, EFT is also available for the remaining instalments.

03What does “share payment with guests” mean?

When you book a stay at your bungalow through the simpleplek membership calendar, you can invite co-guests and split the contribution directly on the platform — an effortless way to share the experience and co-fund stays with friends, family, or travel companions.

04What are the specifications of The Steens and The Fawcett?

The Steens features 2 generous bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, and 240 m² of flowing floor area connecting private garden terraces. The Fawcett is uniquely secluded within the nature reserve amongst milkweed trees, shaped around stillness, privacy, and panoramic coastal views.

05How are payments secured?

All acquisition deposits and installment funds are held securely with an appointed conveyancer trust account in terms of property law until sectional title milestones are achieved, ensuring maximum legal and financial protection. Priority on your preferred bungalow goes to the first person to complete payment, and once the initial booking payment is made, further payments can also be made by EFT.

06How do I meet the team or schedule a private consultation?

You can schedule a private appointment with simpleplek and our architectural advisory team during your stay for a fee of R30k. Book your appointment here.
